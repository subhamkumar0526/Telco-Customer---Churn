# Retail Sales Analytics Report

Period: 2023-01-01 to 2025-12-31

## KPIs
- Total sales: **3,382,947**
- Total profit: **489,406** (margin 14.5%)
- Orders: 6,000 | Customers: 886 | Avg order value: 564

## Key Insights
1. **Growth:** sales by year -> 2023: 938,378, 2024: 1,175,070, 2025: 1,269,498. Latest YoY growth: 8.0%.
2. **Seasonality:** month 10 is the peak; Oct-Dec drive a big share of annual sales -> stock up and plan campaigns before the festive season.
3. **Category:** 'Technology' earns the most profit. Lowest-margin sub-category: Tables (9.8% margin).
4. **Region:** 'West' leads in profit; 'South' has the weakest margin (13.9%).
5. **Discounts hurt:** orders with 31-40% discount have a margin of -30.2%. 11.5% of all orders are loss-making -> cap discounts or restrict them to high-margin items.
6. **Customers:** top 20% of customers generate 54% of revenue.
7. **Champions:** 195 customers bring 41.8% of revenue -> reward with loyalty perks.
8. **At-Risk:** 76 previously good customers have gone quiet -> win-back email / offer campaign.

## Recommendations
- Cap discounts at ~20% and track margin per discount band weekly.
- Run festive-season campaigns early; keep inventory for peak months.
- Build a retention program for Champions and a win-back flow for At-Risk customers.
- Review pricing/cost of the lowest-margin sub-category and weakest region.
